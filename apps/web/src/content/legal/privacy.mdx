This policy describes how the Atlas Screening demonstration environment handles personal data. The environment is provided for evaluation. It does not process real customer data, and the sample tenants, subscriptions, and revenue figures it displays are synthetic.

## 1. Who is responsible

The operator of the demonstration environment is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Questions about it can be sent through the contact page.

## 2. Data we collect

**Demo requests.** When you submit the contact form we store your name, work email, organization, role, industry, message, the time of your consent, and a shortened hash of your IP address that is used only to limit abuse.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we store your email address and the time you confirmed the subscription. We send a confirmation message first, and you are subscribed only after you follow the link in it.

**Accounts.** Users of the demonstration application have an email address, a password stored only as a salted Argon2id hash, an optional authenticator secret stored encrypted, and a record of sign-in events in an audit log.

**Technical data.** The servers record request identifiers, status codes, and timing in logs. Logs do not include passwords, tokens, or request bodies.

## 3. Why we use it

We use demo request data to reply to you and arrange a walkthrough. We use newsletter data to send the notes you asked for. We use account and audit data to provide the service and keep it secure. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for the demo request follow-up and the newsletter, on performance of a contract for account use, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure for audit and abuse prevention.

## 5. Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties for their own purposes. Hosting and email delivery providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. Language models used by the product run on our own infrastructure.

## 6. Retention

Demo requests are kept until the request has been answered and for a limited period afterwards. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Audit log entries are kept for the life of the environment because the log is append-only. Scheduled deletion of other categories of data is not yet implemented, and we will update this policy when it is.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or move your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact us through the contact page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security

We protect data with transport encryption, column encryption for sensitive fields, role-based access, tenant isolation in the database, and a tamper-evident audit log. The security page lists what is and is not claimed.

## 9. Cookies

The site uses only the cookies it needs, plus a local setting that remembers your language and that you have seen the cookie notice. See the cookie policy.

## 10. International transfers

If the service is hosted outside your country, your data may be transferred. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used.

## 11. Changes

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above.
