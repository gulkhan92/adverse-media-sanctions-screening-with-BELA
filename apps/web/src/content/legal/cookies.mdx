This policy explains what the Atlas Screening website stores in your browser.

## What we use

| Name                                          | Purpose                                                            | Duration                            | Type      |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | --------- |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`                                 | Remembers your language choice                                     | 1 year                              | Necessary |
| `atlas_cookie_notice` (local storage)         | Remembers that you saw this notice                                 | Until cleared                       | Necessary |
| `atlas_access`, `atlas_refresh`, `atlas_csrf` | Keep you signed in and protect requests, set only after you log in | Session or up to the session limits | Necessary |

## What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics on this website.

## Your choices

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Doing so signs you out and shows the cookie notice again. Blocking the necessary cookies stops sign-in from working.

## Changes

If we add any non-essential cookie, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
